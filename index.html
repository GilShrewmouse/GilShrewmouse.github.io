About

Hi, I'm Ekaterina! I started my career in gamedev as a community manager (there are a few casual mobile games under my belt, and ArcheAge), but this is about my next step: QA.

In 2020, I joined The Most Games as a QA tester, and since then, I evolved into a senior QA tester, then lead QA, and finally QA manager. Not your usual Pokémon!

I have experience with functional, compliance, and compatibility testing - and, of course, my favorite: localization testing. And I still find time to work on some projects personally - I'd hate to lose my hands-on experience!
